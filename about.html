About CHESTER MOVIE HUB

Introduction

CHESTER MOVIE HUB is a dark, cinematic movie platform inspired by the general experience of modern streaming services. It lets visitors discover movies, read details, watch trailers and keep a personal watchlist.

Project purpose

This website is a group assignment for Principles of Web Design at Limkokwing University of Creative Technology.It is designed to overcome the boredom of the students particularly after writing Exams when their minds need refreshment more than ever

Platform features

Browse & search

Search by title or description and filter by genre with instant results.

My List

Save and remove movies. Your list survives a page refresh.

Details & trailers

Open a details page and watch a trailer in a pop-up player.

Responsive

Works on phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Target audience

  • Students and young adults who enjoy movies and series.
  • Families looking for something to watch together.
  • Anyone who wants a simple, fast catalogue on a phone or computer.
